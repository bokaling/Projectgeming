[TITLE] How a Jet Engine Works
[SCENE] the jet engine in the hangar, the fan spins
NARRATOR: This engine swallows half a ton of air every second.
[SCENE] the engine cut open
NARRATOR: Let's cut it in half. Everything turns on one long shaft.
[SCENE] close on the fan
NARRATOR: At the front, a giant fan pulls the air in.
[SCENE] the bypass air
NARRATOR: Most of that air goes around the core. It makes most of the push.
[SCENE] the compressor squeezes the air
NARRATOR: The rest goes into the compressor. Rows of small blades squeeze it tighter and tighter.
[SCENE] fuel burns in the combustor
NARRATOR: In the combustor, fuel is sprayed in and burns at over 1,500 degrees.
[SCENE] the turbine spins
NARRATOR: The hot gas rushes through the turbine and spins it.
[SCENE] the shafts connect turbine and fan
NARRATOR: Shafts carry that spin back to the fan and the compressor at the front.
[SCENE] the exhaust blasts out
NARRATOR: The hot gas blasts out of the back and pushes the plane forward.
