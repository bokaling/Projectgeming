[TITLE] How a Car Engine Works
[SCENE] the hood opens on the engine
NARRATOR: Your engine sets off 100 small explosions every second.
[SCENE] the engine cut open
NARRATOR: Here it is, cut in half. Four cylinders, four pistons, one spinning crankshaft.
[SCENE] the intake valve opens
NARRATOR: First, the intake valve opens. Air and a mist of fuel rush into the cylinder.
[SCENE] the piston moves up and squeezes the gas
NARRATOR: Then the piston moves up. It squeezes that mix into a space ten times smaller.
[SCENE] the spark plug fires
NARRATOR: Now the spark plug fires. The mix explodes at over 2,000 degrees.
[SCENE] the piston pushes down hard
NARRATOR: The blast pushes the piston down with about three tons of force.
[SCENE] the exhaust valve opens
NARRATOR: Next, the exhaust valve opens. The piston pushes the burnt gas out to the exhaust.
[SCENE] the connecting rod turns the crankshaft
NARRATOR: Each connecting rod turns the crankshaft, just like your legs turn bike pedals.
[SCENE] the four cylinders take turns
NARRATOR: The four cylinders take turns, so there is always one pushing.
[SCENE] the flywheel spins
NARRATOR: A heavy flywheel at the end smooths out every single push.
[SCENE] the whole engine working
NARRATOR: At highway speed, each cylinder does this 25 times a second.
[SCENE] close on the timing belt
NARRATOR: And one thin rubber belt keeps all of it in time. So what happens when it snaps?
