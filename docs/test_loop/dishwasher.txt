[TITLE] How a Dishwasher Works
[SCENE] the dishwasher door opens
NARRATOR: A dishwasher uses less water than washing by hand.
[SCENE] the dishwasher cut open
NARRATOR: Let's look inside while it works.
[SCENE] the inlet valve lets water in
NARRATOR: First, a valve lets in just a few liters of clean water.
[SCENE] the water collects in the sump
NARRATOR: The water collects in a small cup at the bottom, called the sump.
[SCENE] the heater warms the water
NARRATOR: A heating element warms it to sixty degrees.
[SCENE] the wash pump pushes water up
NARRATOR: A pump pushes the hot water up a tube to the spray arms.
[SCENE] the spray arms spin
NARRATOR: The water jets push the arms around, and they blast every plate from below.
[SCENE] the filter catches food
NARRATOR: The water runs back down through a filter that catches the food scraps.
[SCENE] the drain pump
NARRATOR: Then a second pump pushes the dirty water out.
[SCENE] the whole dishwasher working
NARRATOR: So why are the plates still wet when it finishes?
